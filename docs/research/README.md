# Research documents

[Toward spatial audio through matter](modal_reference/paper.md) is the first working
technical paper. It accompanies the dry modal reference study and includes seven
verified references, derivations, computed figures, limitations and a reproducible
validation procedure.

[Configurable dry-structure experiments](modal_reference/parameter_experiments.md)
extends it with eight parameter variations, physical-unit comparisons, saved
configuration replay and explicit solver capability checks.

[Decisions and assumptions](modal_reference/decisions.md) records what the project
owner selected, what is provisional and what remains open before modeling the
curved, water-filled basin. Computed results do not substitute for measurements.

[From dry metal modes to water loading](water_loading/study.md) starts a configurable
water-inertia study with analytical and NGSolve fluid-cell checks. It reports
provisional loaded basin modes, approximation limits and the next spatial-fluid
milestone. It is preserved as the earlier local-inertia approximation.

[A shared wet-basin model](wet_basin/study.md) extends it with nonlocal spatial
fluid inertia, actual simulated pressure and synchronized metal/water playback.
It records separate wet-response convergence criteria, passing evidence through
80 Hz and the failed higher-frequency intervals. Its top remains pressure-release;
gravity/capillary resonances and measured vessel sound are not predicted.
